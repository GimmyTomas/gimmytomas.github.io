About me Research Teaching Olympiads Other

I consider teaching to be an important part of my involvement in academic life. At the IAS there are no students and I have no teaching duty, but during my PhD I have been a teaching assistant many times, and I look forward to the moment I will be back at teaching. I always take the job with extreme enthusiams, but also very seriously. These are the courses I taught during my PhD.

General Relativity

I have been a teaching assistant for the Master's course taught by prof. Daniel Baumann in 2021, 2022 and 2023. This included leading tutorials and writing many of the exercises and the exam problems, which can be found appended to the lecture notes.

Over the years, I have encountered many people curious to learn General Relativity. Some of them are physicists specializing in a different subfield, others simply have a scientific education. Inspired by the teaching experience, I have written a document where I attempt a very hard task: explain an entire course of General Relativity in 10 pages, assuming basically no physics prerequisites at all. You can find it here. Have fun!

GRAPPA Student Seminars

I have been a teaching assistant for the Master's course taught by prof. Gianfranco Bertone in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024. The goal is to introduce the students to research subjects and papers—we used Dark Matter as a topic to serve that purpose. I have lectured on candidates and searches, including: WIMPs, PBHs, axions, ULBs, MOND, indirect detection, direct detection and GW searches.
